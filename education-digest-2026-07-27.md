# AI in Education Digest — 2026-07-27

**Colombia — Universidad de La Sabana, business school economics course** — Times Higher Education profiles a case study in which faculty built an intelligent GenAI dashboard into an introductory economics course, using it as a personal study assistant for concept checks early on, then as a full "teammate" in a group assessment where it gave real-time, personalized feedback objective enough that instructors say it would have been unattainable by hand.
*For educators:* What would change in your grading if an AI could give every student group individualized, real-time feedback — would you trust it as a "teammate," or only as a tool you double-check?
Source: https://www.timeshighereducation.com/campus/genai-teaching-colleague-assessment-case-study

**Hong Kong — Hong Kong Polytechnic University** — Times Higher Education describes PolyU's "TIMS" virtual assistant, a smart chatbot embedded directly inside WhatsApp rather than a standalone app, which offers students real-time lecture summaries and answers audio-based questions as part of the university's "Education 4.0" push to meet students on the platform they already live in.
*For educators:* If your AI support tool lived inside the app your students already check 50 times a day, would more of them actually use it — and is that convenience worth the loss of a dedicated, more controllable learning space?
Source: https://www.timeshighereducation.com/campus/case-omnichannel-ai-assistant

**United Kingdom — Edinburgh Napier University study, 6,000+ students** — Times Higher Education reports on a survey-based study finding most UK students who use AI in their studies are largely honest about it: 67% of habitual AI users said they would not use it on an assignment after being told not to, though 5.2% admitted using it "most of the time" or "always" even when forbidden.
*For educators:* Does this data change how you'd design an AI policy — toward trusting most students with clearer norms, or toward closing the gap for the persistent minority who ignore the rules regardless?
Source: https://www.timeshighereducation.com/news/majority-students-deny-using-ai-when-forbidden-study-finds
