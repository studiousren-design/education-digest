# Education Digest — September 22, 2026

**United States (Special Education)** — Some parents are now feeding their child's individualized education program (IEP) into AI chatbots to translate dense special-education jargon into plain language and to draft home goals that mirror the school's own. Special educators are split: some welcome better-informed, more confident parent advocates, while others worry that AI-drafted requests can sprawl to 20–30 pages, slowing down a process already strained by paperwork and inaccurate AI outputs.

*For educators:* If a parent arrives at an IEP meeting with an AI-generated document longer than the IEP itself, how do you decide what's genuine advocacy and what simply can't be acted on?

**United States (K-12 classrooms)** — Two teachers recount how single student questions reshaped their thinking about AI: a Brooklyn special-education teacher noticed a student skipping the productive "struggle" of learning entirely rather than merely plagiarizing, which she says matters more than the plagiarism itself, while another teacher's search for an answer to an eighth grader's question about why learning still matters led her into a room of software engineers and tech executives who had never met a K-12 teacher.

*For educators:* If AI is quietly removing the "productive struggle" of learning rather than just facilitating cheating, would you even notice it happening in your own classroom?

Source: https://www.edsurge.com/news/two-students-two-questions-that-wouldnt-let-go

**Middle East (Gulf states)** — Gulf universities, led by institutions like the Mohamed Bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence in Abu Dhabi, are betting on AI research investment to expand their higher-education sectors and retain homegrown talent, positioning the region as a "bridge" between Western and Asian AI development rather than a follower of either.

*For educators:* If a country invests directly in AI research capacity instead of importing Western curricula, does that reshape what "keeping up" with AI in education actually means?

Source: https://www.timeshighereducation.com/news/middle-east-bets-ai-research-strengthen-its-universities
