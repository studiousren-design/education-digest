# AI in Education Digest — 2026-08-03

**United Kingdom — London School of Innovation** — Times Higher Education reports that this postgraduate institution has been granted UK degree-awarding powers for a fully AI-native teaching model: students are assigned AI "private tutors" who deliver content as text or via AI avatar, formative work is assessed and given feedback entirely by AI, while human academics remain responsible only for marking summative assessments and exam-board oversight.
*For educators:* If an AI tutor could carry every formative interaction with a student, what would be lost — or gained — by academics only ever seeing that student's final, summative work?
Source: https://www.timeshighereducation.com/news/virtual-lecturers-be-overseen-academics-ai-university

**United Kingdom — higher education sector-wide survey** — Times Higher Education covers new survey data showing most UK university staff don't fear AI will make them redundant within three years, but researchers like UCL's Rose Luckin warn this confidence may be misplaced complacency, since funding cuts and over-hiring corrections are increasingly "rebadged as automation" even when AI isn't the true cause.
*For educators:* If a role at your institution were cut and labelled an "AI efficiency," how would you find out whether AI actually did the work — or just provided cover?
Source: https://www.timeshighereducation.com/news/ai-risk-university-jobs-despite-staff-believing-roles-are-safe

**United States — rural school districts** — EdSurge reports that AI professional development remains scarce in rural districts compared with urban and suburban ones, with researchers at Texas Tech University finding that unreliable broadband, outdated infrastructure, and a lack of AI-specific training leave rural educators largely on their own to figure out tools that could otherwise help with differentiated instruction and IEP drafting.
*For educators:* If your district got AI tools tomorrow but no training budget to go with them, what would you need to teach yourself first?
Source: https://www.edsurge.com/news/in-rural-districts-ai-resources-for-educators-are-scarce
