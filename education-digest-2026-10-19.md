**[United Kingdom / Undergraduate assessment]** — A HEPI survey reported by THE finds that 95 per cent of UK students now use AI in some way, and the share including AI-generated text directly in assessed work has risen to 12 per cent. The headline finding is that nine in 10 undergraduates are now using AI in assessments.
*For educators:* If AI use in assessment is now the norm, what would an assessment look like that makes the student's own thinking visible rather than policing the tool?
Source: https://www.timeshighereducation.com/news/nine-10-uk-undergraduates-now-using-ai-assessments-survey

**[United States / K-12 teachers: Prospect Schools, Brooklyn and Detroit]** — Anthropic launched Claude for Teachers, a free tool for US K-12 educators with a skills library linked to evidence-based curricula mapped to standards in all 50 states. The skills library was piloted with teachers at Prospect Schools in Brooklyn, and Anthropic plans to evaluate it in Detroit Public Schools Community District.
*For educators:* What evidence would you need to see from the Detroit evaluation before trusting a vendor-built teaching assistant with your lesson planning?
Source: https://www.edsurge.com/news/anthropic-introduces-claude-for-teachers

**[Higher education / Chatbot persona "Einstein AI"]** — THE reports on a case in which an "Einstein AI" tool was substantially edited and then taken down on 26 February, spotlighting concerns about chatbots that impersonate historical figures.
*For educators:* When a chatbot speaks as a famous expert, how do students learn to ask whose words and whose judgement they are actually getting?
Source: https://www.timeshighereducation.com/news/strange-case-einstein-ai-spotlights-chatbot-concerns
