# AI in Education Digest — 2026-10-26

> Note: searches surfaced no EdSurge or Times Higher Education AI stories verifiably published in the last 48 hours. The three below are the most recent, specific, not-yet-covered stories found (roughly 1–3 weeks old; exact publication dates were not confirmed).

**[UK / Research funding — CRANE, EPSRC-funded]** — A UKRI-linked research network used AI models to score proposals on seven criteria and rejected about half of 179 applications before any human read them. After researcher backlash it apologised for a lack of transparency, suspended offers already issued and agreed to re-review every screened-out proposal.
*For educators:* If an AI sift is acceptable for triaging grants but not for marking, what exactly makes a first-pass judgement legitimate, and who should have to be told about it?
Source: https://www.timeshighereducation.com/node/745326

**[UK / Higher education assessment — City St George's, University of London]** — Academics built and piloted Duologue, an AI platform that holds a Socratic dialogue with students to map their knowledge and gaps in AI literacy and computational thinking, so assessment runs throughout learning rather than as a final verdict. The aim is to make AI-cheating detection unnecessary, though the piece is written by the platform's builders and no independent evaluation was found.
*For educators:* If assessment becomes a continuous conversation with an AI, what would you still want a human to witness and judge directly?
Source: https://www.timeshighereducation.com/node/745565

**[Germany, Japan, South Korea and beyond / University campuses]** — OpenAI has launched a student ambassador programme with "campus leads" on campuses including in Germany, Japan and South Korea, running until June 2027. Critics, including a UNSW professor, say universities have little choice but to be complicit and that the move deepens a commercial AI company's foothold in higher education.
*For educators:* Who should decide which AI companies get a presence on your campus: the institution, the faculty or the students themselves?
Source: https://www.timeshighereducation.com/news/openai-campus-little-choice-be-complicit
