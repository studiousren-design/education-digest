**[Europe / Doctoral education]** — A European University Association survey of 217 universities found that more than two-thirds say their PhD students use AI in their research, mostly as auxiliary support (49 per cent), with 17.5 per cent reporting use in both conducting research and auxiliary support. Almost a quarter of universities do not know how much their candidates use it, and 38 per cent are drafting AI guidelines for the first time.
*For educators:* If a quarter of institutions cannot say how their doctoral researchers use AI, what would honest supervision of AI-assisted research look like?
Source: https://www.timeshighereducation.com/news/two-thirds-universities-report-ai-use-among-doctoral-students

**[European Union / University assessment]** — The European University Association's Thomas Jørgensen warns that academics informally using tools such as ChatGPT to grade student work could fall foul of the EU AI Act, which sets transparency and data requirements for AI used in assessment. Universities running their own formal AI tools face compliance challenges too, and the Commission's AI Office has yet to issue exact guidelines.
*For educators:* If you use AI to give feedback or marks today, could you explain to a student, or a regulator, exactly how it reached its judgement?
Source: https://www.timeshighereducation.com/news/looming-eu-ai-act-could-force-universities-change-everything

**[United Kingdom / AI degree programmes]** — UK enrolment on AI-specific degrees reached 10,825 students in 2024-25, up 19 per cent in a year and treble the 2019-20 figure, with Hull, Edinburgh and Robert Gordon the largest cohorts. Experts warn content may lack rigour and date quickly, and urge flexible, regularly updated programmes complemented by short modular courses.
*For educators:* If the tools change faster than a three-year degree, what should an AI curriculum teach that will still be true on graduation day?
Source: https://www.timeshighereducation.com/news/flexibility-needed-keep-ai-degrees-relevant-demand-soars
