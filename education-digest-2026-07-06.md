# Education Digest — July 6, 2026

**[Indonesia — Jakarta]** — A Jakarta classroom teacher, David Webb, spent a year building "LibraryAid," an AI app that tracks roughly 30 signals per student (interests, reading history, classroom topics) to generate personalized book recommendations. One student reading two grade levels behind made three times his usual reading progress once the app matched him to a book series he loved.
*For educators:* If a well-tuned recommendation engine can out-perform a teacher's gut instinct on book matching, which other "instinct-based" calls in your classroom might be worth testing against data — and which ones shouldn't be?
Source: https://www.edsurge.com/news/podcast-can-an-algorithm-replace-a-teachers-instinct

**[Hong Kong]** — Hong Kong Polytechnic University built TIMS, an AI chatbot embedded directly into WhatsApp, so students can get round-the-clock answers to enrollment, timetable and admin questions inside an app they already use daily, instead of a separate school portal. The "omnichannel" design reflects a broader regional shift toward meeting students on existing messaging platforms rather than building new ones.
*For educators:* Would your students actually engage with a well-built AI helpdesk living inside the app they check constantly, versus a portal they open once a semester?
Source: https://www.timeshighereducation.com/campus/case-omnichannel-ai-assistant

**[United States — K-12]** — The International Society for Transforming Education released an expanded "Profile of an AI-Ready Graduate," a free framework meant to help K-12 educators translate AI literacy into concrete, assessable skills rather than vague buzzwords, layered onto schools' existing standards instead of requiring a separate curriculum overhaul.
*For educators:* If you had to name three specific, observable things a genuinely "AI-ready" graduate of your school could do, what would they be — and could you assess for them today?
Source: https://www.edsurge.com/news/international-society-for-transforming-education-expands-its-ai-ready-graduate-framework
