# AI in Education Digest — September 9, 2026

**[United Kingdom]** — A Jisc-led national trial has 15 UK universities piloting AI marking and feedback tools, Graide and TeacherMatic, with students informed they are part of the trial and some institutions offering an "active consent" opt-out. Times Higher Education reports organisers insist the goal is supporting academics, not replacing them, even as staff voice concerns about oversight and workload.
*For educators:* If an AI tool marked a stack of your students' work tonight, what would you still need to check by hand tomorrow morning?
Source: https://www.timeshighereducation.com/news/ai-marking-trial-not-looking-replace-humans

**[Singapore]** — At Nanyang Technological University, associate vice-provost Tan Seng Chee piloted a generative AI "learning companion" for teachers that resembles a discussion forum but pushes back on ideas like a thinking partner. Times Higher Education reports educators described it as a powerful brainstorming partner that prompted deeper reflection on their own teaching, rather than simply spitting out generic lesson plans.
*For educators:* Would you rather an AI tool hand you a finished lesson plan, or ask you the questions that help you design a better one yourself?
Source: https://www.timeshighereducation.com/campus/how-support-students-learn-about-and-beyond-ai

**[United States]** — Anthropic launched Claude for Teachers, a free version of its chatbot built for K-12 educators with a library of teaching skills tied to standards in all 50 states, co-developed with Learning Commons and piloted with teachers at Prospect Schools in Brooklyn. EdSurge reports the move puts Anthropic in direct competition with OpenAI and Google for a foothold in classrooms already crowded with district-approved AI tools.
*For educators:* With three major AI labs now courting teachers directly, who should be setting the ground rules for what "built for the classroom" actually means?
Source: https://www.edsurge.com/news/anthropic-introduces-claude-for-teachers
