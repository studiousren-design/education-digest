# Education Digest — 2026-08-26

**[United States — Massachusetts]** — At the America's Youth AI Festival in Boston, hosted by Day of AI, MIT RAISE, AASA and the Edward M. Kennedy Institute, student leaders from across the country acted as "student senators" to draft a first-of-its-kind national AI policy for K-12 classrooms. Their proposal centered on academic integrity, compromised critical thinking, privacy and mental-health risks, deepfakes, and unequal access to AI tools.

*For educators:* If students themselves are asking for guardrails on AI, whose job is it to write the policy — the school, the state, or the students?

Source: https://www.edsurge.com/news/on-ai-policy-students-have-plenty-to-say

**[United States — Detroit]** — Anthropic is piloting Claude for Teachers, a free large language model for verified K-12 educators built around a library of teaching skills and curricula mapped to academic standards in all 50 states, inside Detroit Public Schools Community District. The pilot focuses less on generating lesson plans and more on studying the tool's effect on teacher well-being and everyday practice.

*For educators:* What would you want measured in a classroom AI pilot besides time saved — burnout, trust, or something else entirely?

Source: https://www.edsurge.com/news/anthropic-introduces-claude-for-teachers

**[Hong Kong, Indonesia, Japan, Malaysia, the Philippines, Singapore, South Korea, Thailand]** — A paper presented at Times Higher Education's Asia Universities Summit in Hong Kong, drawing on survey responses from institutions across eight systems, found that only 44 per cent of universities have access to a 5G network — a gap that constrains real-time, mobile and data-intensive AI applications on campus. Researchers argue that sustained AI impact in the region depends on coordinated investment across infrastructure, pedagogy, research capacity, leadership and workforce training, not just access to AI tools themselves.

*For educators:* Before adopting a new AI tool, how often does the conversation start with infrastructure and training rather than the software itself?
