# AI in Education Digest — 2026-10-01

> Note: EdSurge and Times Higher Education pages could not be fetched from this environment, so publication dates could not be confirmed; stories are drawn from search results surfaced as recent.

**[Australia & UK / University admissions]** — A "mystery shopper" study sent about 700 enquiries from six fictitious personas (including a school student, a career changer and a parent) to 30 Australian and 33 UK universities by phone, chatbot, web form and Instagram. Only 37 per cent of replies made the "student" keen to continue, and staff generally performed worse than ChatGPT at making prospective students feel valued.
*For educators:* If an AI answers an applicant's questions more warmly than we do, what exactly is the human contribution we should be protecting?
Source: https://www.timeshighereducation.com/news/indifferent-responses-student-enquiries-costing-enrolments

**[United Kingdom / Higher education students]** — A YouGov survey of 2,373 UK students found that fear of being wrongly flagged by AI detectors is driving stress, with international students twice as likely to report "a lot" of it. More than half cited being accused of cheating when they had done nothing wrong as a stressor.
*For educators:* Is the anxiety created by detection tools a cost we are willing to impose on honest students, and what would assessment look like if we dropped them?
Source: https://www.timeshighereducation.com/news/fear-being-flagged-ai-detectors-drives-stress-among-students

**[China / Research universities]** — Chinese research-intensive universities are being urged to differentiate and orient themselves towards "national strategic needs", aligning teaching with emerging fields such as artificial intelligence, integrated circuits and biomedicine. The reforms are top-down, steering institutional missions rather than leaving them to individual universities.
*For educators:* When national priorities steer curricula towards AI, who protects the disciplines that don't map neatly onto strategic industries?
Source: https://timeshighereducation.com/news/china-urges-universities-differentiate-top-down-reforms
