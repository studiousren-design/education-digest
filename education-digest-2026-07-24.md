# AI in Education Digest — 2026-07-24

**Global — India, Africa, and English-learner households in the US** — EdSurge reports that most AI-powered edtech only translates its interface, not its instructional content, leaving Hindi-medium students in India, families across a continent with over 2,000 languages in Africa, and English-learner households in the US unable to actually use the lessons; the piece contrasts this with California's new mandatory personal-finance course, which will fail the very families most likely to need it if it's never delivered in their home language.
*For educators:* If your instructional materials were only available in your students' second language, how much of what you teach would actually reach them?
Source: https://www.edsurge.com/news/the-language-barrier-is-the-real-barrier-in-edtech

**United States — K-12 classrooms** — EdSurge examines the rising practice of teachers using generative AI to instantly "level down" grade-level texts for struggling readers, arguing this often replaces real scaffolding — graphic organizers, vocabulary sheets, sentence starters — with a permanent ceiling on what those students are ever asked to read. The piece warns this can quietly harden inequities, since some students keep reading original literature while others are handed AI-generated summaries indefinitely.
*For educators:* Are you using AI to help a struggling reader reach grade-level texts, or to permanently avoid the harder work of getting them there?
Source: https://www.edsurge.com/news/we-must-stop-using-ai-to-level-down-our-students

**Saudi Arabia — King Abdulaziz University** — Times Higher Education's Awards Arab World 2026 shortlist features King Abdulaziz University's "AI Education for Non-Specialists," a national model that offers accredited AI courses to students across every discipline, including those with no prior programming experience, aiming to make basic AI fluency a university-wide baseline rather than a computer-science specialty.
*For educators:* What would change on your campus if AI literacy were a graduation requirement for every major, not an elective for technical students?
Source: https://www.timeshighereducation.com/news/awards-arab-world-2026-shortlist-announced
