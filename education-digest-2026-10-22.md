# AI in Education Digest — 2026-10-22

**[United States / K-12 schools]** — An EdSurge essay by Melinda Medina (21 Oct) argues that AI's polished output is breaking the way schools reward high-achieving students, citing an August 2026 Common Sense Media survey in which 70% of US teens said they use AI for schoolwork. Secondary coverage of the piece adds that 39% of students felt they miss learning when they use AI to complete assignments, and the author urges schools to recognise intellectual curiosity rather than finished products.
*For educators:* If a flawless submission no longer signals understanding, what would you reward instead, and how would you see it?
