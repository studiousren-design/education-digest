# Education Digest — 2026-08-25

**[China — Suzhou]** — Academics at Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University, a Sino-British joint venture, published practical safeguards for using generative AI in literature reviews after seeing it produce convincing but fabricated references. Their approach keeps researchers anchored to verifiable sources rather than trusting AI-generated citations at face value.

*For educators:* What checkpoint would catch a fabricated but plausible-looking AI citation before it reaches a student's final submission?

Source: https://www.timeshighereducation.com/campus/safeguards-against-genai-hallucination-literature-reviews

**[United Kingdom]** — The London School of Innovation was granted degree-awarding powers for a model built around AI "private tutors" instead of academics lecturing from the front of a classroom, with the institution's leadership calling it validation of an AI-first approach to higher education. Students are meant to get a personalised, hands-on learning path guided primarily by AI rather than live instruction.

*For educators:* If AI can personalise the "what" and "how" of instruction, what does a human educator still need to own to keep the degree meaningful?

Source: https://www.timeshighereducation.com/news/virtual-lecturers-be-overseen-academics-ai-university

**[United States — English language learners]** — Teachers working with English language learners are experimenting with AI chatbots to give bilingual students more real-time practice and scaffolding than a single teacher can provide across a classroom of varying proficiency levels. Early accounts describe cautious enthusiasm rather than wholesale adoption, with teachers testing where AI genuinely helps versus where it flattens the nuance of language acquisition.

*For educators:* How would you tell the difference between an AI tool that accelerates a language learner's progress and one that just makes their output look more fluent than it is?

Source: https://www.edsurge.com/news/ai-meets-esl-and-teachers-are-intrigued
