# Education Digest — July 16, 2026

**[Sierra Leone / Estonia]** — A new EdSurge analysis highlights a study in which Sierra Leonean secondary teachers, after just one day of AI training, produced math learning gains equivalent to more than a year of extra schooling — while Estonia is deliberately holding AI back from its youngest students so they build foundational skills first before introducing the tools.
*For educators:* If one day of training unlocked a year's worth of learning gains elsewhere, is your school's AI training investment closer to Sierra Leone's minimal-but-targeted model, or is it missing the mark entirely?
Source: https://www.edsurge.com/news/ai-wont-replace-educators-but-it-is-changing-how-students-learn

**[United Kingdom / United States]** — After one Brown University economics exam average collapsed from 96% to 48.6% once it was moved in person, Princeton has ended 133 years of unproctored testing and the University of Bath is rolling out a "Two-Lane" system labeling each assessment as AI-closed or AI-open — though Times Higher Education notes not every institution agrees paper exams are the fix, with Cardiff resisting a wholesale return to invigilated testing.
*For educators:* If proctored exams mainly reward memorization that AI can already replicate, what exactly are you protecting by bringing them back?
Source: https://www.timeshighereducation.com/depth/are-universities-returning-person-exams-combat-ai-cheating

**[United States / United Kingdom / Australia]** — A Turnitin analysis of tens of millions of submissions found that US university students are "offloading" writing to AI at roughly double the rate of their UK and Australian peers, with 19.4% of US work crossing an 80%-AI-generated threshold versus under 11% in the other two countries. The gap suggests national culture, assessment design, or institutional messaging — not just access to the technology — is shaping how much students lean on AI.
*For educators:* If you ran this same analysis on your own course, would you actually want to know the answer?
Source: https://www.timeshighereducation.com/news/ai-usage-higher-among-american-students-finds-turnitin
