# AI in Education Digest — 2026-10-21

**[United Kingdom / Russell Group universities]** — A Bett UK survey of 1,054 undergraduates ("A Degree of Doubt", published 15 Oct) found that 77 per cent of Russell Group students say they are taught little or nothing about AI, against 64 per cent at post-1992 universities. Where AI is taught at Russell Group institutions, 62 per cent of students say it mostly covers how far AI can inform an assignment, and only 20 per cent say its use in the workplace is taught thoroughly.
*For educators:* If your AI guidance only tells students what counts as cheating, who is teaching them what AI does in the job they want?
Source: https://timeshighereducation.com/news/students-report-little-ai-teaching-russell-group-courses
