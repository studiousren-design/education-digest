# AI in Education Digest — 2026-08-12

**United Kingdom — University of Surrey** — Surrey undertook a systematic redesign of every degree programme to embed discipline-specific AI directly into curricula and assessment, shifting grading toward evaluating students' "process" rather than final "output." The overhaul rolls out to the whole student body from September 2026, rather than starting as an isolated pilot module.
*For educators:* If grading rewards how a student got to an answer over the answer itself, what happens to the students who think best in ways that don't show their work?
Source: https://www.timeshighereducation.com/news/surrey-redesigns-all-degrees-embed-discipline-specific-ai

**United Kingdom — London School of Innovation** — The UK's Office for Students granted the London School of Innovation degree-awarding powers for AI-native online master's programmes, where course content is delivered by AI avatar "lecturers" and each student is paired with an individual AI "private tutor" for Socratic-style dialogue. It marks one of the first cases of a regulator formally certifying a degree built around AI-delivered instruction rather than AI as a supplementary tool.
*For educators:* If a regulator can certify an AI "lecturer," what part of teaching is that certification actually vouching for — content delivery, or something else?
Source: https://www.timeshighereducation.com/news/virtual-lecturers-be-overseen-academics-ai-university

**United States — Prospect Schools, Brooklyn & Detroit Public Schools** — Anthropic launched Claude for Teachers, a free tool built for K-12 educators with a library of teaching skills and curricula mapped to standards in all 50 states, piloted first with teachers at Prospect Schools in Brooklyn. A formal evaluation study is now planned in Detroit Public Schools Community District to measure its effect on educator well-being and day-to-day practice, not just adoption rates.
*For educators:* Most AI-in-schools studies measure usage or test scores — what would change if the headline metric were teacher well-being instead?
Source: https://www.edsurge.com/news/anthropic-introduces-claude-for-teachers
