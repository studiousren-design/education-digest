**[United States / New York City and Los Angeles schools]** — New York City announced a one-year moratorium on student-facing generative AI for public elementary and middle school students. Los Angeles Unified has restricted access to generative AI tools on all student-issued devices, across all grades, for the 2026-27 school year.
*For educators:* If the largest districts pause student use while teachers still use AI themselves, what should a school be able to demonstrate before lifting such a ban?

**[United Kingdom / AI-taught courses]** — The London School of Innovation has been granted degree-awarding powers for courses taught with AI, with virtual lecturers to be overseen by academics. It is an early test of whether a regulator will accept AI-delivered teaching under human supervision.
*For educators:* If an AI delivers the lecture and a human only oversees it, which parts of teaching do you think must remain irreducibly human?
